# Vocabulary evidence provenance

The current curriculum is generated by `node tools/expand-coverage.mjs`. There is no fixed total or per-band quota. `reselect-core.mjs` and `build-enrichment.mjs` now call that generator rather than rebuilding the old 241-item selection.

Inputs are checked into `tools/data`: the original v4 curriculum and permanent IDs, historical ID lookup, curated bilingual additions, and page-scoped OCR transcription of the six supplied FY24–FY26 A/B English papers. Each source PDF is identified by SHA-256. The original PDFs remain the authority; the transcription is a bounded, reproducible source for frequency counts, not a claim to include unavailable listening audio.

See [coverage policy](coverage-policy.md) for included sections, inflection rules, prioritisation, and the distinction between printed matches and separately reviewed grammar targets. Counts and additions are summarised in `coverage-audit.json`.

Every old v4 lemma retains its ID, meaning, POS, examples and history. Reactivated historical entries reuse their old ID. The registry is append-only. The original Waseda scheduling, memory, grading and session modules remain pinned and unchanged.

Generated practice examples remain explicitly labelled. No newly written example is presented as a verbatim past-paper quotation.
